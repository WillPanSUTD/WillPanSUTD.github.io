Method for segmenting and denoising triangle mesh

Wei Pan, DAI Chaofan, Xuequan Lu
US Patent 12,536,620
{ } BibTeX
— Summary

This patent covers a triangle-mesh processing method for segmentation and denoising. It is linked to feature-preserving surface cleanup, where the algorithm should suppress noise without washing out sharp or semantically meaningful regions.

This work appears in US Patent 12,536,620.

Algorithm principle

The method operates on triangle-mesh geometry, using segmentation cues to separate feature regions from smooth areas before denoising. That structure-aware step helps preserve surface details during smoothing.

Visual material

Detailed method and result figures are represented on the linked PAT page and related mesh-denoising publication.

Results and impact

The patent is part of the broader mesh-processing workflow used for industrial 3D data cleanup and feature-preserving denoising.
