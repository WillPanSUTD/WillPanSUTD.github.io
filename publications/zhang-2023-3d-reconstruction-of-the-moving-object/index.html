3D reconstruction of the moving object with double-shooting based on phase shifting profilometry

Qinghui Zhang, Hao Li, Lei Lv, Shenglin Lu, Wei Pan
Infrared and Laser Engineering, 52(12):20220891-1
↓ PDF { } BibTeX DOI
— Summary

This paper targets a common limitation of phase-shifting profilometry: moving objects violate the static-scene assumption during multi-frame fringe capture. The double-shooting strategy is used to make phase recovery more robust to motion.

This work appears in Infrared and Laser Engineering, 52(12):20220891-1.

Problem setting

Moving-object reconstruction requires phase information that remains consistent across frames. The method uses double-shooting acquisition to reduce phase inconsistency before 3D reconstruction.
