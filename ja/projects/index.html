プロジェクトと製品

研究プロトタイプ、エンジニアリングプロジェクト、産業製品として展開された3Dビジョンシステムを紹介します。3Dカメラ、SciVision SDK、3Dアルゴリズム基盤、検査・計測ワークフロー、ロボットビジョン応用を含みます。

  • 2025-2026 · OPT Machine Vision · ★ DEVELOPMENT KIT
    SciVision Machine Vision SDK
    OPT's SciVision visual development package: a cross-platform industrial vision SDK covering 2D, 3D, deep-learning inference, measurement, controls, communications, and secondary development APIs.
    productSDKSciVisionmachine vision
  • 2025-2026 · OPT Machine Vision · ★ PRODUCT LINE
    OPT 3D Camera Product Line
    A full industrial 3D camera portfolio spanning laser profile, projected structured light, speckle/RGB-D, and ToF sensing, paired with Smart3 software for rapid deployment in measurement, inspection, and robotic guidance.
    product3D camerasindustrial visionOPT
  • 2025 · OPT Machine Vision · ★ ALGORITHM SUITE
    OPT 3D Algorithm Platform
    A deployable industrial 3D algorithm stack for preprocessing, reconstruction, registration, matching, measurement, defect inspection, robotic guidance, and Smart3 zero-code workflow deployment.
    productalgorithmsSmart3industrial AI
  • 2025 · OPT Machine Vision · ★ RGB-D
    OPT Speckle Structured-Light RGB-D Cameras
    A binocular speckle/RGB-D 3D camera family for large field-of-view depth and color acquisition, with LSA1 series covering wide-area logistics, depalletizing, picking, and millimeter-level positioning.
    productRGB-Dspecklerobotics
  • 2025 · OPT Machine Vision · ★ STRUCTURED LIGHT
    OPT Projected Structured-Light 3D Cameras
    A projected structured-light camera family for one-shot 3D imaging, covering FPB dual-projector and FPC single-projector series with high repeatability, pixel-aligned 2D/3D output, and hardware-side point-cloud computation.
    productstructured light3D reconstructionrobot guidance
  • 2025 · OPT Machine Vision · ★ LASER PROFILE
    OPT Laser Profile 3D Cameras
    A line-laser 3D camera family for high-speed profile scanning, with LPE2, LPF2, and LPH2 series covering 7 mm to 2600 mm scan widths, up to 6400 X-axis profile points, and up to 54 kHz sampling.
    productline laser3D measurementinspection
  • 2019— · Shenzhen University
    3D Autoscan System
    A scanner + rotary platform pipeline that captures arbitrary objects to ~0.1 mm in under 5 minutes—built to seed a 3D shape database without per-object parameter tuning.
    structured lightregistrationC++
  • 2018—2019 · Shenzhen University
    Robotic Pick-and-Place with RGB-D Vision
    A UR5 arm + Dahuan three-finger gripper + Orbbec RGB-D, with a perception stack that fuses learning-based grasp planning with classical geometry for robust 3D pick-and-place across varied industrial parts.
    roboticsgrasp planningRGB-D
  • 2012 · Zhejiang University · ★ NATIONAL OPT 1ST PRIZE
    Laser Spy: Acoustic Localization via Reflected Laser
    An undergraduate optical-design build—silicon photovoltaics, dual-channel ADC, and a LabVIEW pipeline for waveform separation, time-delay estimation, and acoustic source localization, all from laser light reflected off a vibrating window. Won 1st prize at the National Optoelectronic Design Competition.
    opticsDSPLabVIEWundergrad
