OPT Laser Profile 3D Cameras

A line-laser 3D camera family for high-speed profile scanning, with LPE2, LPF2, and LPH2 series covering 7 mm to 2600 mm scan widths, up to 6400 X-axis profile points, and up to 54 kHz sampling.

Precision 3D volume measurement of transparent adhesives via spectrally optimized line laser scanning and enhanced centroid extraction

Spectrally optimized line laser scanning combined with an enhanced centroid extraction algorithm enables precision volume measurement of transparent adhesives, overcoming the light-scattering artifacts that corrupt conventional laser profilometry.

Jan 1, 2025
Gap measurement method based on projection lines and convex analysis of 3D point clouds

A geometric method combining projection-line fitting and convex analysis on 3D point clouds precisely measures gap dimensions in assembled components, providing a robust alternative to contact gauging for automated industrial inspection.
