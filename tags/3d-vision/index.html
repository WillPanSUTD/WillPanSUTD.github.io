3D Vision

Few-Shot 6D Object Pose Estimation via Decoupled Rotation and Translation with Viewpoint Encoding

A few-shot monocular 6D pose method that estimates rotation and translation separately: a viewpoint encoder trained only on synthetic images retrieves rotation from a codebook of rendered views, and a GDR-Net-based branch regresses translation from dense geometric features with 600 real images per object.

Jan 1, 2026

3D Surface Matching Based on Adaptive Threshold and Curvature Map

This work focuses on 3d surface matching based on adaptive threshold and curvature map, contributing to Wei Pan's research thread in 3D vision, optical metrology, geometry processing, and industrial inspection.

Jan 1, 2026

Calibration Method of Fringe Projection Based on High-Order Polynomial

A fast calibration method for phase-shifting profilometry: a high-order polynomial maps pixel coordinates and absolute phase to 3D, and its coefficients are fitted with a sparse greedy randomized Kaczmarz (SGRK) algorithm instead of least squares.

Jan 1, 2025

WTAYOLO: A Detection Model for Weak Scratches on Metal Surfaces

WTAYOLO is a weak-scratch detection model for metal surfaces, targeting low-contrast defects that are difficult to separate from texture and illumination variation.

Jan 1, 2024

High-precision three-dimensional data real-time progressive rendering method and system

A patent for real-time progressive rendering of high-precision 3D data, aimed at interactive visualization of large point clouds and industrial 3D measurements.

Jan 1, 2022

Sequencing in Transformation of Rasterized 3D Models

Ph.D. thesis on the sequencing problem in shape transformation of rasterized 3D structures, studied through three cases: pick-and-place of voxels, pick-and-place of LEGO bricks, and folding of voxel chains.
