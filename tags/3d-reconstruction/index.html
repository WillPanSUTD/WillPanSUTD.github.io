3D Reconstruction

Learning-based reconstruction of moving transparent objects

A learning-based reconstruction method for moving transparent objects in phase-shifting profilometry: a diffusion model separates the fringe patterns superimposed by reflections from the front and rear surfaces, and the moving object is then reconstructed based on the SIFT algorithm and two-dimensional motion equations.

Jan 1, 2026

Automatic geometric calibration and unwarping of rotating line-scan imaging systems using elliptical distortion of circular patterns

Rotating line-scan cameras and laser profilers operate under a sector-scan geometry, which introduces systematic geometric distortions in both 2D intensity images and 3D line-scan height profiles.

Jan 1, 2026

OPT Projected Structured-Light 3D Cameras

A projected structured-light camera family for one-shot 3D imaging, covering FPB dual-projector and FPC single-projector series with high repeatability, pixel-aligned 2D/3D output, and hardware-side point-cloud computation.

Dec 8, 2025

3D reconstruction of the moving object with double-shooting based on phase shifting profilometry

A phase-shifting profilometry method in which the camera captures each projected fringe pattern twice, extracts phase under the resulting mixed phase shift using tracked object motion, and reuses images to raise the reconstruction frame rate of moving objects.

Jan 1, 2023

3D reconstruction of moving object by double sampling based on phase shifting profilometry

A trigger device makes the camera capture two images for every projected fringe pattern; a least-squares phase model that uses the tracked object motion then recovers the phase from three consecutive images, raising the frame rate of phase-shifting profilometry for moving objects.

Jan 1, 2023
