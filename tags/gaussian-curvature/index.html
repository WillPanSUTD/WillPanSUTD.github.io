Gaussian Curvature

Gaussian Curvature Filter on 3D Meshes

The Gaussian Curvature Filter (GCF) lowers the Gaussian curvature of triangle meshes without computing it: each vertex moves just far enough that a 1-ring neighbour reaches its tangent plane, and an optional geometric constraint keeps features while removing noise.
