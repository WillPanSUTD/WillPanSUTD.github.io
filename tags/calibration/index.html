Calibration

TTA-MAC: Per-Instance Test-Time CAD-Anchored Refinement of Monocular Depth Calibration and 6D Object Pose

A test-time refinement layer for RGB-only 6D object pose: it jointly refines the two monocular-depth calibration scalars and the 6D pose against a CAD-derived signed distance field, with a heavy-tailed prior and a trust region that rejects wrong-basin states.

Jan 1, 2026

Depth-adaptive calibration of line-laser 3D measurement systems using rational polynomial mapping and depth-independent misalignment compensation

A two-stage calibration for line-laser 3D measurement: a 22-coefficient rational polynomial mapping fitted across the full depth of field, plus a depth-independent inverse rotation that removes the shear caused by sensor-to-motion misalignment.

Jan 1, 2026

Calibration Method of Fringe Projection Based on High-Order Polynomial

A fast calibration method for phase-shifting profilometry: a high-order polynomial maps pixel coordinates and absolute phase to 3D, and its coefficients are fitted with a sparse greedy randomized Kaczmarz (SGRK) algorithm instead of least squares.

Jan 1, 2025
