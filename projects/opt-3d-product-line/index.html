OPT 3D Camera Product Line

A full industrial 3D camera portfolio spanning laser profile, projected structured light, speckle/RGB-D, and ToF sensing, paired with Smart3 software for rapid deployment in measurement, inspection, and robotic guidance.

OPT’s 3D camera portfolio is organized around the major industrial sensing routes: laser profile cameras for scanning and micrometer-level measurement, projected structured-light cameras for one-shot high-precision reconstruction, speckle/RGB-D cameras for large field-of-view depth and color capture, and ToF cameras for high-frame-rate perception.

Product matrix covering laser profile, projected structured light, speckle structured light, and ToF cameras.

My role

My role is centered on the algorithm and software layer that turns 3D camera data into deployable inspection workflows: reconstruction, calibration, point-cloud/mesh processing, measurement, defect detection, registration, robot guidance, and Smart3 integration.

Product architecture

The product line is designed as a hardware and software suite rather than a set of isolated sensors. Camera families cover close-range high-precision measurement, large-format depth acquisition, 2D/3D aligned inspection, and robot guidance. The Smart3 algorithm platform connects the camera output to visual workflow design, point-cloud processing, measurement tools, and production deployment.

The portfolio compares laser triangulation, temporal coded structured light, and binocular speckle depth sensing.

Technology routes

Laser profile cameras emphasize mature motion-assisted measurement with um-level precision. Projected structured light emphasizes one-shot reconstruction and high repeatability for static or indexed parts. Speckle/RGB-D cameras emphasize wide field-of-view capture, RGB-depth alignment, and cost-effective logistics or robot-vision use cases.

Different routes are positioned by speed, precision, and application scenarios.

System deployment

The camera line is paired with the Smart3 visual algorithm platform, allowing inspection workflows to be configured with camera acquisition, preprocessing, point-cloud reconstruction, measurement, defect detection, and user-defined visualization.

Hardware: OPT 3D camera series. Software: Smart3 algorithm platform.

Industry coverage

The product line is positioned for lithium batteries, 3C electronics, photovoltaic, semiconductor, automotive, robotics, food, medicine, chemical, and warehouse automation scenarios, with applications including glue inspection, PCB measurement, welding inspection, dimensional checking, robot guidance, and volume measurement.

Industrial application coverage across more than 30 market segments.
