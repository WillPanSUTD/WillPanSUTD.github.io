Paper-Conference

SuP: Sub-cloud Driven Point Cloud Registration

SuP turns low-overlap point cloud registration into high-overlap sub-cloud anchor mining, using DSAM, OPS, MPN, and merge-to-match to recover reliable correspondences from ambiguous partial scans.

Jan 1, 2026

Self-calibrating structured light three-dimensional imaging method with fringe projection

A self-calibrating fringe-projection 3D imaging technique that integrates bundle adjustment: starting from the independent calibration of the camera and projector, it jointly optimises the camera and projector parameters together with the scene's 3D points, so that extrinsic parameters drifting under vibration are corrected without interrupting measurement.

Jan 1, 2026

Learning-based reconstruction of moving transparent objects

A learning-based reconstruction method for moving transparent objects in phase-shifting profilometry: a diffusion model separates the fringe patterns superimposed by reflections from the front and rear surfaces, and the moving object is then reconstructed based on the SIFT algorithm and two-dimensional motion equations.

Jan 1, 2026

3D Surface Matching Based on Adaptive Threshold and Curvature Map

A 3D workpiece surface matching method that uses an adaptive threshold on a surface curvature map to keep only high-curvature feature points, then matches coarse-to-fine with an integral volume descriptor and ICP.

Jan 1, 2026

Volume Measurement Technology of Dispensing Transparent Adhesives Based on Line Laser Scanning

A line-laser scanning system that measures the volume of transparent dispensed adhesive, using a green-yellow substrate to absorb the violet laser light that passes through the bead and a scored candidate-point method for stripe-centre extraction.

Jan 1, 2025

SFRF6D: Selective Fewer-Reference Fusion for 6D Pose Estimation

SFRF6D is a model-free 6D pose estimation framework that needs no CAD model and only a few RGB-D reference images with known poses, combining reference-view rendering, geometry-guided sparse cross-view attention and coarse-to-fine pose selection for robustness under occlusion.

Jan 1, 2025

Photometric stereo-based defect detection for lithium battery top covers

Detecting surface defects on lithium battery top covers is critical for ensuring their performance, reliability, and longevity.

Jan 1, 2025

GCMA6D: Graph Convolution and Cross-Modality Attention Fusion for 6D Pose Estimation

GCMA6D is an RGB-D 6D pose estimation network that adds 3D graph convolution to the point-cloud branch, Large Kernel Attention to the image branch, and cross-modality attention with Squeeze-and-Excitation reweighting to fuse the two, for occluded and low-texture objects.

Jan 1, 2025

FPD: Fringe Photometric Deflectometry when Fringe Meets Photometric Stereo

FPD fuses Phase Measuring Deflectometry (PMD) and Photometric Stereo (PS) with per-pixel weights computed from the specular and diffuse reflection components, so that PMD dominates on shiny regions and PS on diffuse ones, for surface reconstruction and defect detection.

Jan 1, 2025

A Reversible Grayscale Method Based on Bit-Field Multi-Channel Fusion Encoding

A non-learning reversible grayscale method that converts an RGB image to HSV and packs quantized hue, saturation and value bit fields into a single 16-bit grayscale image, which decodes back to RGB with simple bitwise operations.

Jan 1, 2025
