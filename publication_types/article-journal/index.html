Fusing Phase Map Servoing and MPC for High-Precision Robotic Tracking of Dynamic Objects

A phase-map visual servoing and model predictive control framework for dynamic robotic tracking, reducing median closed-loop latency from 12.9 ms to 8.3 ms and tracking 0.1 m/s motion with 0.05 mm final error.

Underwater structured-light 3D imaging method based on FP-DiffNet

FP-DiffNet restores degraded underwater fringe patterns with diffusion-model denoising, reaching 42.42 dB PSNR, 0.0354 rad wrapped-phase MAE, and sub-0.1 mm reconstruction RMSE in high turbidity.

TTA-MAC: Test-Time Adaptive Metric Calibration with a Signed-SDF Trust Region for Monocular 6D Object Pose Alignment

A test-time refinement layer for RGB-only 6D object pose: it jointly refines the two monocular-depth calibration scalars and the 6D pose against a CAD-derived signed distance field, with a heavy-tailed prior and a trust region that rejects wrong-basin states.

A few-shot monocular 6D pose method that estimates rotation and translation separately: a viewpoint encoder trained only on synthetic images retrieves rotation from a codebook of rendered views, and a GDR-Net-based branch regresses translation from dense geometric features with 600 real images per object.
